Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Presto ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

Account information (optional). You can use the App without an account. If you sign in, we receive your email address — either from the address you enter for a sign-in link, or from Google when you use "Continue with Google". We also store an account identifier and a sign-in session token so you stay signed in.

Device identifier. The App generates a random identifier for your device and sends it with requests. It is used to keep your activity (posts, likes, saved items, notifications) tied to you while you are signed out, and to carry that activity over when you sign in. It is not an advertising identifier and is not used to track you across other apps or websites.

Content you create. We store what you choose to post or send through the App: posts, comments, likes, saves, reposts, follows, direct messages, submitted programs, and the display name attached to them. Your display name and profile avatar are shown to other users.

Usage information. We record basic interactions with the App, such as which programs are opened, in order to show counts and to rank content. These records are tied to the account or device identifier described above.

Notifications. We store notifications generated for you (for example, likes, comments, follows, and messages) and whether you have read them.

We do not collect your phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or files. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your information.

How We Use Information

We use the information above to operate the App: to sign you in, to display your content and other users' content, to send you notifications, to send sign-in emails, to keep content counts and ranking working, and to prevent abuse.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

Google (sign-in with Google)
Resend (delivery of sign-in emails)
Cloudflare (hosting, database, and file storage for the App)

Data Storage and Security

Data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure. All traffic between the App and our servers is transmitted over HTTPS. Direct messages are visible only in the conversation between you and the other participant. Profile avatars are stored on your device. Like any service, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your content and account information while your account or device identity is in use. You can delete your own posts, comments, and messages in the App. To request deletion of your account and the information associated with it, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
