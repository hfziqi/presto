2. How we access, use, store and share data from your Google Account

You can sign in to Rokowe with "Sign in with Google". When you do, this is exactly what happens with your Google data:

  • Access. During sign-in Google gives us an ID token containing your email address and a Google account identifier (the subject ID). We request no other Google permissions: we do not request and cannot access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive files, photos, or any other Google data. We never receive your Google password.
  • Use. We use that email address and account identifier for one purpose only: to create your Rokowe account, identify you, sign you in, keep you signed in, and let you sign in again on another device. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to running the App.
  • Store. Your email address and the Google account identifier are stored in the App's database (hosted on Cloudflare), linked to the account and the content you create. We do not store your Google password, and we do not store Google access tokens or refresh tokens.
  • Share. We do not share your email address or account identifier with anyone, except the infrastructure providers listed in section 4 that host the App, and we never sell it. Other members of the App never see your email address — only the display name you choose.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to serve advertising, and we do not allow humans to read it except (a) with your consent, (b) for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or (c) as required by law.

3. How we use information

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in
  • To store, display and deliver the content you create and receive
  • To send you sign-in emails when you use email sign-in
  • To send you in-app notifications
  • To count and rank content
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train machine-learning models.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that run the App, and only as needed to operate it:

  • Google — for "Sign in with Google" (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Resend — to deliver sign-in emails to the address you type (Resend Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudflare — hosting, database, file storage and delivery (Cloudflare Privacy Policy)

Other members can see the content you publish and the display name attached to it. Direct messages are visible only to you and the person you are talking to. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Cookies and local storage

The App does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. It stores a small amount of data on your own device (your sign-in session, your display name and avatar, and local settings). Clearing the App's data removes it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and content while your account or device identity is in use. Specifically:

  • Sign-in requests and expired sign-in sessions are deleted automatically when they expire or when a newer request replaces them
  • Content you delete is removed from the App; like most services we may keep short-lived backups for a limited period
  • If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and the content tied to it, and we confirm when it is done

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete content: you can delete your own posts, comments and messages inside the App at any time
  • Delete your account and data: email us at the address below and we will delete your account and the data associated with it
  • Access or correction: email us and we will tell you what we hold about you and correct it if it is wrong
  • Use the App without an account: browsing and playing do not require signing in
  • Stop collection: uninstalling the App stops all further collection from your device

We answer these requests within 30 days. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the account owner.

8. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers uses HTTPS. Data is stored on our hosting provider's infrastructure, and access to it is restricted to what is needed to run the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International transfers

Our service providers (listed in section 4) may process and store data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union. They apply their own safeguards for such transfers.

10. Children's privacy

Rokowe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
