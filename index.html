Presto

Create small programs. Share them. Play what others made.

What Presto is

Presto is a mobile app for people who like to make and share small interactive programs — mini games, toys, and experiments. You can publish a program, post about it, and let anyone open it and play it right inside the app.

What you can do in the app

  • Create and publish small programs that run inside the app
  • Play programs made by other people
  • Post, comment, like, save and repost
  • Follow the people you like
  • Send direct messages to other users

Signing in

You can browse and play without an account. If you sign in, you can do it with an email sign-in link or with Google. We ask for your Google email address only to create your account and keep you signed in — we do not read your Gmail, your contacts, your files, or anything else in your Google Account. You can also use the app as a guest, with a random identifier generated on your device.
