Presto

Create small programs. Share them. Play what others made.

What Presto is

Presto is a mobile application where people make and share small interactive programs — mini games, toys and experiments that run right inside the app. Anyone can open a program someone else published and play it immediately, without installing anything extra.

Presto is also a small community built around that: every program has an author, and members can post, comment, follow each other and send direct messages.

Who it is for

Presto is for people who enjoy making small things and trying other people's work: hobbyist game makers, designers, students, and anyone who likes to play something new. The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

What you can do in the app

  • Create a small program and publish it for others to play
  • Play programs published by other members
  • Post, comment, like, save and repost
  • Follow the people whose work you like
  • Send direct messages to other members

Why the app asks for your Google account

Presto uses Sign in with Google only to identify you and create your account. From Google we receive your email address and a Google account identifier, and nothing else.

We do not read your Gmail, contacts, calendar, files or photos, and we request no Google permissions beyond basic sign-in — we never receive your Google password. You can also create an account with an email sign-in link instead, or use the app as a guest with no account at all: browsing and playing do not require signing in.
